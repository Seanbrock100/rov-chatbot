# Hercules MK3 — Missing Drawings

Generated by audit of drawing-tree.html vs /work documents filesystem.

> **MK3 Build Note:** The MK3 was an **electronics upgrade only** — the hydraulic system and frame/structure are mechanically identical to earlier Hercules variants (MK2, 15-30 Mk3). Drawings from older build classes are therefore **fully valid** for the MK3 hydraulic and structural systems.

---

## 📄 Manual Scan — TMA00974 (Hercules Series Hydraulics, Rev C2, 30/07/2009)

Scanned all 144 pages of `Hercules Series Hydraulics TMA00974.pdf` (Section 17 = embedded drawings).

**Verdict: The hydraulic drawings embedded in TMA00974 are valid for the MK3.** The older number prefixes (ROV-0211, ROV-0148, ROV-0226, ROV-0300-D-0440, ROV-0305-D-0400) reflect the same unchanged hydraulic hardware. These are usable reference drawings for the MK3 build.

However, the specific MK3 sub-assembly drawing numbers listed in the drawing tree (ROV-0300-D-04xx, ROV-0311-D-04xx, ROV-0249-D-013x series) are still not present in this manual — those numbered revisions must be retrieved from the vault if the drawing index needs to reference them by those exact numbers.
